## 2. Core Voice & Tone Invariants

1. **Calm, Unhurried, and Grounded**:
   - Zero manufactured urgency (*«Успейте до конца недели!»*).
   - Zero empty tech buzzwords (*revolutionary, seamless, game-changing, groundbreaking, intuitive AI*).
   - Clear distinction between facts, working tools, and unvalidated hypotheses.
2. **Action & Facts Over Process Nouns (Инфостиль)**:
   - Use active verbs instead of heavy Russian nominalizations (*«осуществление оптимизации» → «убираю рутину»*).
   - Name concrete artifacts: *таблицы, статусы в чатах, знание в голове одного диспетчера, второй выезд инженера, расчет тарифа*.
3. **Respect for Reader Attention**:
   - Explicitly acknowledge reader time: *«Не буду перегружать пост ссылками...»* / *«Спасибо за уделённое время!»*.
   - Never dump walls of raw links in community chats.

## 3. Channel Heuristics

### A. Telegram Peer Communities (Global Talent, Startup Pools, Alumni)
- **Greeting**: Always open with a warm, peer-level greeting (*«Всем привет.»* or *«Привет, сообщество!»*). Never jump abruptly into an ask.
- **Identity**: Always anchor who is speaking in the first 2 lines (*«Я Саша Катин, занимаюсь дизайн-инженерией...»*). In vetted communities, peer credibility is the primary trust driver.
- **Format**:
  - Pithy 3-step operational breakdown (1. Нахожу затык $\rightarrow$ 2. Собираю минимальную систему $\rightarrow$ 3. Проверяемый результат).
  - Clean link handling: 1 primary hub URL (`groundworkops.co.uk`) + clean reference to personal research code (`github.com/lessthanzero`). Avoid dumping 4+ raw links that generate multiple preview cards.
  - Thread/DM flexibility: Always support both (*«напишите в личку (или здесь в тред)»*).
  - Reciprocal closing: Invite direct contacts and warm intros alike (*«Буду благодарен и за личный контакт, и за рекомендацию: „Знаю ребят, у которых именно этот процесс буксует“»*).

### B. B2B Operational Outbound (Groundwork)
- **"Walk The Last Bad Job"**: Never open with software features or generic AI pitches. Focus on one recent, expensive failure:
  - *«Show me a recent job, dispatch, or handoff that was painful to price, schedule, or complete.»*
- **Peer Operational Vocabulary**:
  - Logistics: *consolidated trunk runs, ad-hoc pallet quotes, tail-lift constraints, vehicle payload limits*.
  - Field Trade / HVAC: *first-time fix rate, van stock mismatches, repeat callouts, job docket delays*.
- **The Bounded Offer**: Always offer a low-risk, bounded first step (an £850 diagnostic audit or a 1-workflow pilot) rather than an open-ended expensive software build.

### C. Direct Introductions & Mentorship
- Direct, appreciative tone (*«Тема, привет! Я Саша Катин, и твой контакт дал мне...»*).

## 4. Anti-Patterns to Flag & Eliminate

- ❌ Starting community posts with an abrupt commercial pitch (*«Ищу клиентов на...»*).
- ❌ Overloading chat posts with multiple raw URLs that generate stacked preview cards.
- ❌ Blurring open-source/research code with client case studies (always distinguish: *«исследовательский код пишу сам»*).
- ❌ Hiding sole-trader/visa status in the UK (in UK tech circles, being a registered sole trader is an asset).
